    ABOUT · NAVAYUGA GROUP

    Nation-scale civil engineering,
    from Hyderabad to every river basin

    Navayuga Engineering Company Ltd is the EPC flagship of the Hyderabad-based Navayuga Group, founded by C. Visweswara Rao. Part of the group that built the Krishnapatnam deep-water port, major river bridges, national highways and tunnels across India.

    At Polavaram, Navayuga is head-works contractor — spillway, spill channel, earth-cum-rock-fill dam and associated structures — the components that command 50 lakh cusecs of Godavari flood.

    • 48 radial gates
    • 960 MW hydropower
    • 194 TMC gross storage
    • 1.5 km diaphragm wall
